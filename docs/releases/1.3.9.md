# Voltura Markdown 1.3.9

Press **F5** in Reader mode to reload the current file from disk, even after dismissing the file-change notice. Reload restores your reading position where possible.

## Downloads

- **VolturaMarkdown-Setup-1.3.9-win-x64.exe** installs or updates the app for your Windows account.
- **vmd.exe** runs as a portable app.

Both executables are self-signed and timestamped. Windows may show a publisher warning.
