# Voltura Markdown 1.3.7

## Improvements

- Right-click highlighted text or press **Ctrl+C** to copy it in the reader and raw-source views.
- **Selection copied to clipboard** appears for two seconds after either gesture succeeds.
- Right-click an unselected link to open **Copy link**.

## Downloads

- **VolturaMarkdown-Setup-1.3.7-win-x64.exe** installs or updates the app for your Windows account.
- **vmd.exe** runs as a portable app.

Both executables are self-signed and timestamped. Windows may show a publisher warning.
